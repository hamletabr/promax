HOW TO ADD PORTFOLIO PHOTOS
===========================

1. Make a folder here for each job, e.g.:
     public/images/portfolio/willow-glen-mitsubishi/
2. Copy your photos into it (jpg or png; resize to ~1600px wide max /
   under ~500 KB each for a fast site).
3. Open  src/data/portfolio.ts  and add a project entry pointing at the
   photos — full instructions are at the top of that file.

The first image listed for a project becomes its cover photo.
