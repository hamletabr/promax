=============================================================
  ADDING JOB PHOTOS TO THE WEBSITE
=============================================================

  1. Make a folder in here. Name it after the job, in normal
     English:

         Campbell furnace swap
         Los Gatos Mitsubishi 3 zone mini split
         Saratoga tankless

  2. Drag the photos into it. Any photos, any names, any size.

  NEVER name a folder after the customer's street ("Beverly Dr").
  That would publish their address next to photos of their home.
  The site refuses folders named like that.

  That's it. There is nothing else to fill in.


-------------------------------------------------------------
IF YOUR PHOTOS ARE IN THE PHOTOS APP
-------------------------------------------------------------

Make the job folder here FIRST, then:

  Open Photos, select the photos for that job (click the first,
  then shift-click the last, or Cmd-click to pick them out),
  and DRAG them onto the folder.

That's the whole export. If dragging is awkward, use the menu
instead: File > Export > Export [N] Photos, set Photo Kind to
JPEG and Size to Full Size, then pick the job folder.

Don't worry that the exported files all get today's date - the
real date the photo was taken is stored inside the photo, and
the site reads that.


-------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IT DOES FOR YOU
-------------------------------------------------------------

From the folder name it works out which tab the job goes under
and which brand badge to show. From the photos it works out the
date and shrinks them for the web.

So "Campbell furnace swap" lands under Furnaces, in Campbell,
dated from the photos.

Photos show in the order their names sort, which for phone
photos (IMG_4821, IMG_4822...) is the order you took them.
Rename them 01, 02, 03 ONLY if you want a different order. The
first photo is the cover.


-------------------------------------------------------------
THE ONE WORD THAT MATTERS
-------------------------------------------------------------

Put one of these in the folder name so it knows the job type:

    furnace           mini split / ductless      heat pump
    air conditioner   water heater / tankless    ductwork
    rooftop           VRF / VRV                  zoning

Add "3 zone" or "multi zone" for multi-room mini splits.
Add the city and the brand if you want them shown:

    "Los Gatos Mitsubishi 3 zone mini split"

If you leave the job type out, the photos still go up - they
just land under "More Projects" instead of the right tab.

Photos dropped in here loose, with no folder at all, still show
up. They group together under "Recent Work".


-------------------------------------------------------------
IF YOU WANT TO WRITE A PROPER DESCRIPTION
-------------------------------------------------------------

Optional, and only worth it for your best jobs - a written
description helps that job show up on Google.

Put a file called info.txt in the job folder with any of these
lines. Anything you leave out is worked out automatically:

    title: 96% AFUE Furnace Swap
    city: Campbell, CA
    brand: Bryant
    summary: Replaced a 20-year-old furnace with a two-stage
      96% AFUE unit. New plenum, sealed flue, done in a day.


-------------------------------------------------------------
LAST THING
-------------------------------------------------------------

Photos don't appear on the public website by themselves. The
site has to be rebuilt and uploaded. Tell whoever handles the
website that new photos are in.
