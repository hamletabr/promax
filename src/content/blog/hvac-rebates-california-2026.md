> **Update (July 2026):** TECH Clean California's rebate funding is currently **exhausted statewide**, so TECH incentives are not available right now. The federal 25C tax credit, HEEHRA, and utility programs below are still active. We monitor TECH funding waves — if the program reopens, we apply it to eligible quotes automatically.

California has been paying homeowners to switch from gas equipment to efficient electric heat pumps — but the programs have confusing names, different rules, and funding that comes and goes. Here's the plain-English guide we walk our San Jose customers through, with the current status of each program.

## TECH Clean California — currently out of funding

TECH Clean California offered contractor-applied incentives on qualifying **heat pumps** and **heat pump water heaters**, taken directly off your invoice. As of mid-2026, the program's funding is **used up** and new applications aren't being accepted.

Two things worth knowing: funding has historically returned in waves, and the program's pause doesn't affect anything else on this list. As a TECH-enrolled contractor we get notified when funding reopens — if it does, eligible quotes get the rebate applied automatically, no action needed on your side.

## Federal 25C tax credit — up to $2,000 per year (active)

The federal Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit (25C) gives you back **30% of the installed cost, up to $2,000 per year**, for a qualifying heat pump or heat pump water heater. Qualifying high-efficiency ACs and furnaces can earn up to $600. You claim it on your tax return; we provide the manufacturer certificates and itemized invoice your tax preparer needs.

Smart timing tip: because the cap resets each year, some homeowners install a heat pump in December and a heat pump water heater in January to claim the credit twice.

## HEEHRA — the big one for income-qualified households (active)

The Home Electrification and Appliance Rebate program (HEEHRA) offers **point-of-sale discounts up to $8,000** on heat pumps for income-qualified households. If your household income is under the area threshold, this can cover a huge share of the project. We check eligibility during the free estimate — it takes minutes and surprises a lot of people.

## Utility & regional extras (varies)

PG&E, Silicon Valley Clean Energy, and San José Clean Energy periodically offer additional incentives — duct sealing, smart thermostats, panel upgrades. These come and go; we track what's live so your quote reflects everything you can claim.

## How to stack what's available now

A qualifying San Jose heat pump project in 2026 can still combine: the federal 25C credit ($2,000 back at tax time) → HEEHRA point-of-sale discounts if income-qualified → any live utility extras. On top of that, [financing](/financing) spreads the remainder into monthly payments — often close to what the energy savings cover.
