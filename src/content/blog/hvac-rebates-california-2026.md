HEEHRA's income-qualified single-family funding is **fully reserved statewide** — we can add eligible households to a waitlist and apply it automatically if funds return. California has been paying homeowners to switch from gas equipment to efficient electric heat pumps — but the programs have confusing names, different rules, and funding that comes and goes. Here's the plain-English guide we walk our San Jose customers through, with the current status of each program.

## TECH Clean California — currently out of funding

TECH Clean California offered contractor-applied incentives on qualifying **heat pumps** and **heat pump water heaters**, taken directly off your invoice. The program's funding is **used up** statewide and new applications aren't being accepted.

Two things worth knowing: funding has historically returned in waves, and the program's pause doesn't affect anything else on this list. As a TECH-enrolled contractor we get notified when funding reopens — if it does, eligible quotes get the rebate applied automatically, no action needed on your side.

## Federal 25C tax credit — expired for installs after 2025

The federal Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit (25C) used to give homeowners back 30% of the installed cost, up to $2,000 per year, for a qualifying heat pump or heat pump water heater. That credit **ended for installations completed after December 31, 2025** — it is no longer available for a 2026 project, and we can't apply it to your quote.

If you installed qualifying equipment before the end of 2025, the credit still applies to that tax year; talk to your tax preparer about documentation. For anything installed from here forward, the savings now come from live utility programs and financing, which we walk through below.

## HEEHRA — fully reserved statewide, waitlist available

The Home Electrification and Appliance Rebate program (HEEHRA) offers point-of-sale discounts on heat pumps for income-qualified households. As of early 2026, **HEEHRA's single-family funding is fully reserved statewide** — new point-of-sale discounts aren't available right now. We can add income-qualified households to a waitlist and apply the rebate to the project automatically if funds return.

## Utility & regional extras (varies)

PG&E, Silicon Valley Clean Energy, and San José Clean Energy periodically offer additional incentives — duct sealing, smart thermostats, panel upgrades. These come and go; we track what's live so your quote reflects everything you can claim.

## What this means for your project in 2026

With 25C expired and HEEHRA reserved, the utility programs above are the incentives actually available today — and they change often enough that it's worth checking at the time of your estimate rather than assuming what applied last year still does. On top of whatever's live, [financing](/financing) spreads the rest into a monthly payment, often close to what the energy savings cover.
