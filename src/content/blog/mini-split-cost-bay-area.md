Search "mini split cost" and you'll find wildly conflicting numbers — $3,000 on one site, $20,000 on another. Both can be right. As a Mitsubishi Diamond Contractor installing these systems across San Jose every week, here are the real ranges we see in 2026 and what actually moves the price.

## Single-zone systems: $4,500–$8,000 installed

One outdoor condenser, one indoor head, one comfortable room. This is the go-to for garage conversions, ADUs, home offices, sunrooms, and that one bedroom that's always too hot. Price varies with:

- **Capacity** (6k–24k BTU) and efficiency tier
- **Line-set length and routing** — a head backing onto the same wall as the condenser is cheapest; long runs across the house cost more
- **Electrical work** — a new 240V circuit or a subpanel adds cost
- **Brand tier** — Mitsubishi hyper-heat costs more than value brands, and lasts longer

## Multi-zone systems: $12,000–$25,000+

One outdoor unit feeding 2–8 indoor heads, each with its own remote and temperature. A whole-home ductless setup for a 3-bedroom San Jose house typically lands in the **$15,000–$22,000** range installed — competitive with a ducted system replacement once you remember every room gets independent control.

## What most quotes conveniently leave out

Permits and HERS testing (required in California), condensate management, line-set covers that don't look like an afterthought, and proper mounting pads. Our quotes include all of it — a lowball number that grows on install day isn't a deal.

## Rebates make a real difference

Mini splits are heat pumps, so they qualify for whatever heat-pump incentives are active — in 2026 that mostly means [utility rebates](/financing) from SVCE, Peninsula Clean Energy or PG&E, which change by city and season. The federal 25C tax credit ended for installations after December 31, 2025, single-family HEEHRA funding is fully reserved statewide (we can add you to the waitlist), and TECH Clean California is exhausted. We check every active program at your free estimate and apply anything you qualify for.
